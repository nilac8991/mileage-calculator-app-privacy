Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

In short. Chilometrico works offline and without an account. Your vehicles, trips and settings stay on your device (and in your own Android backup, if you have it enabled): the developer never receives them. It has no analytics and no servers of its own. The only personal data involved is for ads (Google AdMob), which follow your consent choices. A one-time purchase removes the ads.

This policy explains how data is handled when you use the Chilometrico Android application (package it.nilac.mileagecalculator, the "App"). It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("GDPR") and the Google Play User Data policy.

1 Data controller

The data controller is Daniel Neamtu, independent developer of the App, based in Italy. For any privacy question or request, write to play.neamtu@gmail.com.

2 Data stored on your device

To work, the App saves some information locally on your device, in its private storage:

  • the vehicles you add (make, model, reference ACI rate);
  • the trips you save (kilometres, dates, name, calculated amount);
  • your settings (theme, table year, export preferences, automatic table updates);
  • whether you purchased "Remove ads";
  • when a full-screen ad was last shown, so ads are not shown too often.

None of this data is sent to the developer or to anyone else, and it is not linked to your identity. It is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data in Android Settings, except for any copy in your Android backup (see below). Files you export (PDF/Excel) are created on the device and leave it only if you choose to share them.

Android backup. If backup is enabled on your device, Android may include the App's data in your personal Google account backup and restore it when you reinstall the App or set up a new device. This backup is managed by Google under your account; the developer has no access to it. You can turn it off or delete it in Android Settings (Google → Backup).

ACI tables. The tables used for the calculation come from public data published by ACI and the Italian Official Gazette. They are bundled in the App and, when the device is online, updates are downloaded from a public archive hosted on GitHub. The request contains no personal data from the App; like any web server, GitHub receives your device's IP address to deliver the files (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). You can turn off automatic updates in the App's Settings.

3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • a banner at the bottom of some screens;
  • occasionally, a full-screen "app open" ad when you return to the App after leaving it;
  • occasionally, a full-screen interstitial ad at natural pauses, such as after saving a trip or exporting.

Full-screen ads are limited in frequency. No ads are shown during the first-run introduction. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google and its partners may process:

  • the device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • technical information such as device model, operating system, language, IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • interactions with ads (impressions and clicks) and diagnostic data about ad performance;
  • where available and enabled on your device, Android Privacy Sandbox signals (such as Topics and ad attribution).

Where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. Your choice decides whether you see personalised or non-personalised ads, or no ads at all where consent is required and not given.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from the App, under Settings → Privacy → "Ads consent (GDPR)" (shown where consent is required). You can also limit the use of this data by:

  • resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads);
  • buying "Remove ads", which stops all ad requests.

For details, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4 Google Play: purchase and ratings

The App offers a single one-time "Remove ads" purchase, with no subscription and no account. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. The App never receives or stores your card or payment details. It only receives confirmation from Google Play that you own the purchase, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase after a reinstall. Payment data is governed by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

The App may occasionally ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review feature. Your rating and review go to Google Play and are not visible to the App.

5 What the App does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs besides Google AdMob as described above.
  • No account, sign-in, contacts, GPS location, camera, microphone or access to your files.
  • No servers of its own: the developer receives none of your data.
  • No selling of personal data.

6 Legal basis

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): personalised ads and the related use of device storage and identifiers, where required.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and basic ad measurement, to fund the free version of the App.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): handling the "Remove ads" purchase through Google Play.

7 Retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Any Android backup copy is kept in your Google account according to your backup settings. The developer holds no copy. Data processed by Google for ads and payments is retained according to Google's policies.

8 Transfers outside the EU

Google and GitHub may process data outside the European Economic Area. Such transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

9 Security

Your data is kept in the App's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. Communication with Google services (ads, Google Play) and with the table archive uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and downloaded tables are integrity-checked before use.

10 Children

The App is a tool for calculating mileage reimbursements. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect their data.

11 Your rights

Under Articles 15–22 GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, to object to processing and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the App keeps its data on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it (and deleting any Android backup). For ad-related data you can also use the controls in section 3 or contact Google. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, such as the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali or the authority in your country.

12 Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the App changes. Changes are published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

13 Contact
